About Acebook

A simple place to connect, share and keep up with the people who matter to you.

What is Acebook?

Acebook is a social platform designed to bring people together. Whether you're sharing an update, keeping up with friends or discovering what others are getting up to, Acebook gives you a simple way to stay connected.

We believe social media should be easy to use, enjoyable and focused on connecting people. That's why we've kept Acebook straightforward, with the features you need without unnecessary complexity.

Our Mission

Our mission is to make staying connected simple. Acebook is built around the idea that sharing everyday moments can help people stay close, even when they're miles apart.

What can you do on Acebook?

Share Posts

Share your thoughts, updates and moments with your friends and followers.

Connect with Friends

Find friends and build your own network of people you want to stay connected with.

Discover

Keep up with what's happening and discover new posts from people in your network.

Stay Connected

Keep all your connections in one place and stay up to date with the people who matter to you.

Built by our team

Acebook was created as a team project, bringing together developers with a shared goal of building a social platform from the ground up.

From designing the interface to building the backend and database, every part of Acebook has been developed with collaboration, learning and good software practices in mind.
